KEYCOFFER

Privacy Policy

Local vault. No cloud account. This policy explains what KeyCoffer stores on your device - and what it does not collect.

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Overview

KeyCoffer is an offline password vault for iPhone. It encrypts credentials on device. KeyCoffer does not operate user accounts, cloud sync, advertising networks, or analytics backends for vault contents.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Encrypted credential entries (title, username, password, website, notes, category)
  • A salted master-password verifier in the iOS Keychain (This Device Only)
  • Local preferences such as clipboard clear interval and auto-lock timeout

Vault files are written in the app sandbox with complete file protection. Encryption uses AES-GCM. The encryption key is derived from your master password with PBKDF2 using Apple platform cryptography APIs.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No upload of passwords, usernames, notes, or vault files
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with advertisers
  • No email signup, social login, or developer-operated account
  • No third-party analytics SDK that reads vault contents

4. Clipboard

When you copy a password, KeyCoffer places it on the system pasteboard and schedules an automatic clear after the interval you choose. Pasteboard behavior is also governed by iOS; KeyCoffer cannot override system paste prompts.

5. Locking

KeyCoffer can auto-lock after idle time and soft-locks when the app enters the background by clearing the in-memory session key.

6. Master Password

Your master password stays on device. KeyCoffer cannot recover vault data if you forget it. There is no remote reset.

7. Children

KeyCoffer is a general productivity utility (Age Rating 4+). It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal data online, because it does not collect online account data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date in the in-app privacy document will be updated. Use the same text in App Store Connect's Privacy Policy field.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the App Store product page support channel for KeyCoffer.
